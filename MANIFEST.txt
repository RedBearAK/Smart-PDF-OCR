WHAT CHANGES

  Every run now ends with a timing line on stderr, printed just before the
  corrections count:

      timing: rasterize 11.2s, recognize 96.4s (1.2s/page), correct 0.9s, total 113.1s

  Phases that did not run are simply absent -- a --cached run reports only
  correct and total. The per-page recognition rate is the portable number:
  it holds its meaning across documents of different lengths, so machines
  and --workers sweeps compare directly. The clock is time.monotonic(),
  started at the top of _run; total therefore includes everything, including
  --pdf-out writing, while the named phases cover the three big blocks.

  No flags, no behavior changes otherwise.

  test_cli_paths.py gains test_timing_line_reports_the_run: a cached run

      timing: rasterize 0.1s, recognize 9.7s (2.4s/page), correct 0.4s, total 10.2s
      timing: correct 0.0s, total 0.0s        (--cached)

SUGGESTED FIRST USE

  One sweep answers the remaining performance question with numbers:

      for n in 1 2 4 6; do smart-pdf-ocr doc.pdf -o /tmp/t$n.txt --workers $n --force; done

  and compare the s/page figures. If 6 beats 4 on the M3, the core bound
  (cores // 2) is worth revisiting; if 4 and 6 tie, it is confirmed.
