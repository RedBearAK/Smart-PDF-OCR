WHAT CHANGES

  The change report prints by default. --report remains accepted (now a
  no-op for compatibility with saved commands); --no-report silences the
  report. --quiet is untouched and keeps its single meaning: the per-page
  progress line. The two switches stay orthogonal on purpose -- liveness
  narration and content reporting are different things to want off.

  The GalaxSea invocation is now, in full:

      smart-pdf-ocr PDF_FILE

  the refusal is the guard working. Standalone 21/21; full suite 136
